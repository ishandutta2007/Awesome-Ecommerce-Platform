# Awesome-Ecommerce-Platform

## Top Ecommerce Platform Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Storefront Management, Headless Commerce & Enterprise B2B/B2C Operations*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Ecommerce Platforms**. These tools enable merchants and enterprises to build, manage, and scale online storefronts—handling product catalogs, checkout flows, order management, pricing, and multi-channel commerce experiences.



**Examples** include Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Adobe Commerce (Magento), SAP Commerce Cloud, commercetools, BigCommerce Enterprise, Shopify Plus, Spryker, Elastic Path, VTEX, and Shopware (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom extension development, and transparent ecommerce operations—ideal for merchants, developers, and agencies building tailored commerce solutions without vendor lock-in. The open-source ecommerce landscape spans full-featured platforms (Magento Open Source, Shopware, Sylius), headless API-first frameworks (Saleor, Medusa), and niche solutions for B2B and marketplace scenarios.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Salesforce Commerce Cloud](https://www.salesforce.com/products/commerce-cloud/)**  

  Enterprise commerce platform offering distinct B2C and B2B/D2C solutions on the Salesforce platform, with Agentforce AI integration, native order management, and composable storefront options (PWA Kit, SFRA, Storefront Next) . Pricing is enterprise-tier and typically custom-quoted.



- **[Adobe Commerce (Magento)](https://business.adobe.com/products/magento/magento-commerce.html)**  

  Enterprise version of Magento with advanced B2B features, AI-powered merchandising (Adobe Sensei), multi-database architecture, managed cloud hosting, and 24/7 support . Licensing is GMV-based with industry estimates starting around $22,000/year on-premise and $40,000/year on cloud for smaller merchants .



- **[SAP Commerce Cloud](https://www.sap.com/products/crm/commerce-cloud.html)**  

  Enterprise commerce platform deeply integrated with SAP ERP and Business Suite. The new Cloud ERP Edition (2026) offers preconfigured commerce processes for midmarket and standard enterprise deployments, unified payments, and AI-assisted B2B self-service . Pricing is enterprise-tier and custom-quoted.



- **[commercetools](https://commercetools.com/)**  

  API-first, composable commerce platform built for enterprise flexibility. Offers B2C, B2B, and B2X commerce products, Frontend framework, and hubs for payments, solutions, and AI . Named in Forrester Wave™ Q3 2026 for Commerce Solutions. Best suited for organizations with technical teams building custom commerce experiences.



- **[BigCommerce Enterprise](https://www.bigcommerce.com/enterprise/)**  

  Open SaaS platform with strong native B2B capabilities (price lists, quote management, customer segmentation), headless architecture, and unlimited API calls . Enterprise pricing starts around $1,499/month with GMV thresholds determining plan tiers .



- **[Shopify Plus](https://www.shopify.com/plus)**  

  Enterprise tier of Shopify with flat monthly pricing (~$2,300/mo at moderate volume), removed third-party gateway fees, B2B features (company accounts, net terms), multi-store expansion, and headless support via Hydrogen . Best fit for high-volume DTC and B2B merchants outgrowing standard Shopify.



- **[Spryker](https://spryker.com/)**  

  Composable, API-first B2B commerce and marketplace platform recognized in Gartner® Hype Cycle™ 2026 for Digital Commerce (API-Based Digital Commerce, Modular Commerce, Composable Commerce, Enterprise Marketplaces) . Designed for complex products, multi-tier distribution, and multi-region operations.



- **[Elastic Path](https://www.elasticpath.com/)**  

  Composable commerce platform specializing in B2B, subscriptions, and complex catalogs. Features unlimited pricebooks, account hierarchies, dynamic bundles, B2B2C support, and AI-powered search/agents. Claims sub-30ms average API response time .



- **[VTEX](https://vtex.com/)**  

  AI-native enterprise commerce suite trusted by 3,500+ customers across 40+ countries. Combines Commerce Platform (B2B/B2C/OMS), CX Platform (conversational commerce, discovery), and Ads Platform (retail media) on a multi-tenant SaaS architecture .



- **[Oracle Commerce Cloud](https://www.oracle.com/commerce/)**  

  Enterprise commerce suite tightly integrated with Oracle's broader stack (ERP, CPQ, CX). Offers strong B2B account management and automation within the Oracle ecosystem. High subscription floor ($180K–$600K+ annually per third-party estimates) makes it viable primarily for organizations already standardized on Oracle .



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Magento Open Source](https://github.com/magento/magento2)**  

  The free, self-hosted edition of Magento (Adobe Commerce). Full-featured ecommerce platform with a massive extension marketplace, WYSIWYG page builder, and complete codebase access. Best for SMBs, developer-led teams, and merchants who want full control over hosting and customization . Open Software License 3.0.



- **[Shopware](https://github.com/shopware/shopware)**  

  Modern open-source ecommerce platform popular in Europe, with a powerful administration interface, extensibility via plugins/apps, and a strong B2B feature set. Available in both community (free) and commercial editions. MIT licensed core.



- **[Saleor](https://github.com/saleor/saleor)**  

  High-performance, headless, GraphQL-first ecommerce platform built for composable architectures. Offers a modern Python/Django backend with a separate React dashboard and storefront toolkit. BSD-3-Clause licensed.



- **[Medusa](https://github.com/medusajs/medusa)**  

  Open-source headless commerce engine built with Node.js. Provides modular commerce primitives (products, carts, orders, payments, fulfillment) with an extensible architecture. Ideal for developers building custom storefronts with Next.js or other frontend frameworks. MIT licensed.



- **[Sylius](https://github.com/Sylius/Sylius)**  

  Open-source ecommerce platform built on Symfony (PHP). Features a flexible resource-based architecture, powerful promotion engine, and clean separation of concerns. MIT licensed and particularly strong for B2B and complex catalog scenarios.



- **[WooCommerce](https://github.com/woocommerce/woocommerce)**  

  The most widely deployed open-source ecommerce plugin for WordPress. Transforms WordPress into a full storefront with products, orders, taxes, shipping, and a vast plugin ecosystem. GPL licensed. Best for merchants already using WordPress who need commerce functionality without a separate platform.



- **[PrestaShop](https://github.com/PrestaShop/PrestaShop)**  

  Open-source ecommerce solution with a strong presence in Europe. Features a back-office administration panel, multi-store support, and a marketplace of modules/themes. Open Software License 3.0. Available in both self-hosted and cloud-hosted options.



- **[OpenCart](https://github.com/opencart/opencart)**  

  Lightweight open-source ecommerce platform with a simple admin interface and extension marketplace. GPL-3.0 licensed. Popular among small-to-medium merchants seeking a straightforward self-hosted solution.



- **[Bagisto](https://github.com/bagisto/bagisto)**  

  Laravel-based open-source ecommerce framework with a modern admin panel, multi-currency/multi-locale support, and headless API capabilities. Open Software License 3.0. Gaining traction as an accessible alternative to Magento.



- **[Spree Commerce](https://github.com/spree/spree)**  

  Open-source ecommerce platform for Ruby on Rails. Offers a modular architecture, REST API, and a stable foundation for custom commerce applications. BSD-3-Clause licensed. Historically significant in the Rails ecosystem.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Vue Storefront** — Open-source headless PWA storefront for ecommerce that works with Magento, Shopify, commercetools, and other backends.

- **OroCommerce** — Open-source B2B commerce platform built on Symfony, with native CRM and ERP integration capabilities.

- **Drupal Commerce** — Ecommerce framework built on Drupal, ideal for content-heavy commerce sites with complex editorial workflows.

- **Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Medusa** or **Saleor** for headless backends with **Next.js** or **Nuxt** storefronts, and integrate **Stripe** for payments. For B2B, **Sylius** or **OroCommerce** provide native account hierarchies and pricing structures.
